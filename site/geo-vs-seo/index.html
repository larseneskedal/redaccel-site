GEO vs SEO: what's the difference, and which do you need?

Updated July 2026
Extract redaccel.com · comparison · July 2026

SEO earns a page a position in a ranked list of links; GEO earns a brand a mention inside the AI-generated answer itself. The two overlap far less than most marketers assume: only about 12% of URLs cited by AI engines rank in Google's top 10 for the same prompt[1]. Ranking well no longer implies being cited, and being cited doesn't require ranking at all.

Does ranking #1 in Google get you cited by ChatGPT?

Usually not. The overlap between AI citations and Google's top 10 varies sharply by engine: Perplexity is closest to classic search at roughly 28% overlap, ChatGPT sits around 8%, and Gemini around 9%[1].

Read more

The one exception is Google AI Overviews, which by design summarize what Google already ranks.

Everywhere else, engines cite the page that answers the question most directly, wherever it ranks, and often on a domain you don't control, like a Reddit thread or a review site. Reddit alone is the most-cited domain across major engines[5].

When should you prioritize GEO?

  • Your buyers ask for recommendations: "best X for Y" categories (software, services, consumer picks) are decided inside answers now.
  • You're the challenger: you can't outrank an incumbent's decade of backlinks, but you can out-answer them; ~95% of citations are long tail[2].
  • Your funnel starts with research: high-consideration purchases where a founder asks Perplexity before ever clicking a link.

When should you keep prioritizing SEO?

  • Transactional queries still convert for you: "buy", "near me" and navigational searches still resolve in link lists.
  • You already rank and it pays: don't dismantle working acquisition to chase a new channel; add GEO alongside.
  • Your market barely uses AI answers yet: it's shrinking as a category, but it exists. Check before spending; that's literally what our free audit shows you.

Can one agency do both?

Plenty claim to. Our honest scope: Redaccel does GEO, measurement, earned citations, owned-content extraction, and does not sell link-building or technical SEO retainers. Where the disciplines overlap (structured content, schema, front-loaded answers), the same work serves both, and we'll tell you when a problem is an SEO problem we're not the right ones to fix.
