Redaccel vs NoGood for AI search optimization

Updated November 2026 3 min read By Redaccel
Extract redaccel.com · answer · November 2026

NoGood is a New York growth marketing agency that runs answer engine optimization (AEO) as one service among paid, social, SEO, CRO and creative, for clients like AWS, MongoDB and Nike, with retainers it says average above $20,000 a month[1]. Redaccel is an AI visibility and Reddit marketing agency from Oslo that does one job: getting a brand named when buyers ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI answers for a recommendation. It starts with a free audit of the pages those engines already cite for your buyers' questions, works only those pages, and reports share of voice in AI answers, with a day-90 point where you can walk. Engagements typically start around $2,000 a month[3]. Written by Redaccel, bias declared.

When NoGood is the better pick

If you need paid media, creative, CRO and SEO run together, and AI answers are one part of a larger growth plan, a full-stack agency like NoGood makes sense. Its client list (AWS, MongoDB, Oura, L'Oreal, Nike) shows the scale it is built for, and it uses Goodie AI, an answer engine optimization platform, to track how brands appear in AI answers[1]. If AI search is a line item inside a seven-channel plan, one agency for all seven is simpler.

When Redaccel is the better pick

  • You want AI answers fixed, not a whole marketing program. Redaccel only does AI visibility and the Reddit work that feeds it. There is no paid media team to keep busy.
  • You want to see the target before you pay. The audit lists the exact URLs ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews cite for your buyers' questions. You keep the list whether you hire us or not.
  • Your budget is not enterprise. Engagements typically start around $2,000 a month; the price follows your niche and what its placements cost[3].
  • Reddit matters in your category. In our tracking of GEO and agency questions, Reddit is one of the most cited sources in AI answers[2]. Redaccel came out of Reddit marketing, so the threads the engines already quote are worked by people who know those subreddits and their rules.

How the work runs

  1. Audit. Your buyer questions run through ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI answers, and every cited page is recorded.
  2. Plan. The pages that decide your answers become the target list, with two options and their price.
  3. Work. Placements on those pages only: Reddit threads, best-of lists, review profiles, articles and YouTube.
  4. Report. Monthly share of voice in AI answers against the competitors you name, per engine. At day 90 you can walk if nothing moved.

What to ask any NoGood alternative

  • "Which pages decide my answers today?" An agency that cannot name the URLs the engines cite for your questions is guessing where to work.
  • "How will you report it?" Rankings and traffic do not show whether ChatGPT recommends you. Ask for share of voice on a fixed set of buyer questions, per engine, the same set every month.
  • "What happens at day 90?" Movement usually shows within 6 to 10 weeks (see how long GEO takes). If an agency will not commit to a check point, ask why.
  • "Who does the Reddit work?" Comments from new accounts get removed. Ask whose accounts post and how long their comments stay up.

Proof

Peptide Bureau, our public case study, was built from zero on Reddit alone. Its AI visibility on its buyer questions is up more than 300 percent since the campaign began, on the same fixed prompt set[3].
