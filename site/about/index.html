About Redaccel

A founder-run agency that came out of Reddit marketing and now works every source AI engines cite. Every campaign starts with an audit, and we only work the pages the engines already read for your niche.

Extract redaccel.com · about · October 2026

Redaccel is a founder-run GEO agency. We get brands cited in AI answers across every source LLMs read. Our expertise comes from Reddit marketing for paying clients, and Reddit is the single most cited source in LLM answers. Earning visibility inside the internet's strictest moderation taught us which content survives scrutiny. That is now the whole game.

Where we come from

Reddit only, at first.

Redaccel started as a Reddit marketing agency. Not "social media with Reddit included", Reddit only: earning visibility inside communities that are famously hostile to marketing.

Read more

Working Reddit-only teaches you things no course sells. Moderators read intent, not keywords. There is a sharp line between a contribution a community accepts and a placement it smells instantly. Every placement that survived taught us what real usefulness looks like at the sentence level. That education is the asset this agency runs on.

Why GEO

Reddit became the source.

Reddit became the most cited domain in AI answers[2]. The thing we had gotten good at was never "posting on Reddit". It was getting brands into the consensus that AI models read and repeat.

Read more

Reddit is one room in that house. ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI answers also draw on review platforms, Wikipedia, YouTube, forums, trade press and brands' own sites. So we aimed at the whole target. Reddit remains a core channel, the biggest single one.

The difference

Trained where the judge reads intent.

Many GEO agencies are former SEO consultants whose instincts were formed where the algorithm was a formula. Ours were formed where the judge was reading for intent.

Read more

That is the world all of marketing has entered: platforms now moderate with LLMs[1], and AI engines choose citations the way a sceptical reader decides what to trust. "What survives scrutiny" is no longer a Reddit question. It is the question.

How we run

Four rules we work by

Audit before anything

No campaign starts without the audit. It finds the pages already being cited, and those are the only pages we work.

One KPI

Share of voice in AI answers. If it is not moving by month three, the report says so in plain language.

Exclusivity

One brand per set of buyer questions. We cannot honestly maximise two clients' share of the same answers.

Honest limits

GEO takes 6 to 10 weeks, Reddit is volatile, and nobody can guarantee a specific citation. Clients hear that before they get an invoice.

What does Redaccel do?

Redaccel is a generative engine optimization agency. It measures which sources AI engines cite in a client's category, then earns the client presence in those sources: owned pages, placements, directory and list inclusion, and founder-led community participation. The KPI is share of voice in AI answers against the client's own day-zero baseline. More on what we do.
