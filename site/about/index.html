About Redaccel

Updated July 2026 · Founder-run, Lisbon
Extract redaccel.com · about · July 2026

Redaccel is a founder-run agency in Lisbon that spent the last couple of years doing Reddit marketing for paying clients, and in 2026 shifted entirely to AI visibility — getting brands cited in LLM answers. The Reddit years weren't a detour; they were training. Operating inside the internet's strictest moderation is what taught us which content survives — and surviving scrutiny is now the whole game.

The Reddit years

Redaccel started as a Reddit marketing agency — not "social media with Reddit included", but Reddit only. For a couple of years that was the entire business: earning visibility for clients inside communities that are famously, sometimes gleefully, hostile to marketing. We ran campaigns for Kalshi, the regulated prediction-market exchange; for Roobet, in one of the most heavily-scrutinized categories online; for GPM Music Group in music licensing; and for a creator-management platform, among others.

Working Reddit-only teaches you things no course sells. You learn that moderators read intent, not keywords. You learn the difference between a contribution a community accepts and a placement it smells instantly. You learn by losing posts — watching work get removed and understanding precisely why. Every placement that survived taught us what genuine usefulness looks like at the sentence level. That education is the asset this agency runs on.

Why we changed — honestly

Two things happened at once, and pretending otherwise would be spin.

First, the risk side: in July 2026, Reddit confirmed it uses LLM-based systems to detect coordinated promotion, removing about 25,000 spam posts and comments a day — and applying removals retroactively[1]. Reddit-only marketing became structurally unsafe as a business. Not harder — unsafe. A service whose deliverables can be deleted en masse by a policy change isn't a service you can honestly sell.

Second, the opportunity side: while that was happening, Reddit became the most-cited domain in AI answers[2]. Which meant the thing we'd actually gotten good at was never "posting on Reddit" — it was getting brands into the consensus that AI models read and repeat. Reddit was one room in that house. ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI answers draw on review platforms, Wikipedia, YouTube, forums, trade press and brands' own sites too.

So in 2026 we made the shift completely: Redaccel now focuses entirely on LLM citations — the full surface, not one platform — because it gives clients broader, more durable results than any single-platform play ever could. We didn't abandon our expertise. We finally aimed it at the whole target.

What the Reddit training is worth now

Most GEO agencies are ex-SEO consultants. Competent people, many of them — but they've never had a post removed, never operated where a moderator's judgment decides whether their work exists tomorrow. Their instincts were formed where the algorithm was a formula to satisfy.

Our instincts were formed where the judge was reading for intent. That's precisely the world all of marketing just entered: platforms now moderate with LLMs[1], and AI engines decide what to cite the way a skeptical reader decides what to trust. "What survives scrutiny" is no longer a Reddit question. It's the question. We have years of tuition in it, paid in removed posts.

How we run

  • Founder-run, deliberately small. Based in Lisbon, working with a limited number of clients at a time — the placement work doesn't scale like ad spend, and we don't pretend it does.
  • One KPI: share of voice in AI answers. If it isn't moving by month three, the report says so in plain language.
  • Exclusivity: one brand per buyer-prompt set. We can't honestly maximize two clients' share of the same answers.
  • The honest limits, always stated: GEO takes 6–10 weeks, Reddit is volatile, and nobody can guarantee a specific citation. Clients get that sentence before they get an invoice.
