Free AI visibility audit

Updated July 2026 · Run by a human, free, no call required
The promise concrete and time-bound

We'll show you which AI engines mention you, which mention your competitors instead, and the exact pages those answers are pulled from. Back to you in 48 hours.

Five engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Mode and Google AI Overviews — queried with the prompts your buyers actually type in your category. You get a short document, not a sales deck: mentions per engine for you and each competitor, the specific cited pages driving those answers, and the two or three moves we'd make first. If your AI visibility is fine, the audit says so and we won't chase you.

No mailing list, no drip sequence. You get the audit, and one follow-up asking if you want to talk about it. That's the entire funnel.

What the audit contains

  • Mention map: for ~15–25 category prompts, which of the five engines mention your brand, and whether it's a recommendation or a passing reference.
  • Competitor contrast: the same map for the competitors you name — this is usually the uncomfortable page.
  • Source trace: the exact URLs the engines cite for those answers. This is the actionable part: it tells you which Reddit thread, review page or article is currently deciding your category's answers.
  • First moves: the two or three highest-leverage actions, whether or not you hire us for them.

Why free?

Because the audit is the sales pitch. If the mention map shows competitors being recommended where you're absent, no further persuasion is needed; if it shows you're fine, we'd rather tell you now than churn you in month two. Roughly, doing this well takes us a few focused hours — it's the cheapest honest marketing we know how to do.

Frequently asked

Is it really 48 hours?
48 hours on business days. Submit Friday evening Lisbon time and it lands Monday–Tuesday.
Will you email my competitors?
No. Competitor names are used only inside your audit queries.
Do I need to prepare anything?
Only the form fields above. If you have existing prompts you care about ("we keep losing the 'best X for Y' question"), add them to the competitors field — we'll include them.
