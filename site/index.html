Why it matters

AI answers are written from a small set of pages. If you are not on them, you are not in the answer.

Engines do not show ten blue links. They read a handful of threads, reviews and lists, then name a few brands. Ranking well on Google does not put you on that list.

Questions

Before you ask for the audit

What is GEO, in one sentence?

Generative engine optimization is the work of getting your brand named and cited inside AI answers, by being present on the pages those engines read. The longer answer.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. The audit is our sales pitch: if it shows competitors recommended where you are absent, no further persuasion is needed. If it shows you are fine, we would rather tell you now.

What does it cost after the audit?

Engagements typically start around $2,000 a month, depending on your niche and how contested its answers are. Your audit includes the specific offer for your category. Three-month minimum, then month to month. How pricing works.

How long until something moves?

Usually 6 to 10 weeks. Engines re-read and re-weigh sources on their own schedules, and new placements need time to be found and trusted. If share of voice is not moving by month three, the report says so in plain words.

Do you only do Reddit?

No. Reddit is the largest single source in most categories, so it is a core channel, but we work every source the engines cite: review platforms, lists, trade press, forums, video and your own site.
