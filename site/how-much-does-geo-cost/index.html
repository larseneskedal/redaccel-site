How much does a GEO agency cost?

Updated November 2026 4 min read By Redaccel
Extract redaccel.com · answer · November 2026

A GEO agency is usually priced as a monthly retainer, and at Redaccel engagements typically start around $2,000 a month, depending on your niche. The number itself says less than what it buys: the audit that decides which pages to work, placements on the pages the engines already cite, your own pages rebuilt so they can be quoted, and a monthly share-of-voice report on a fixed set of buyer questions. Tracking alone, bought as software, runs from about a hundred dollars a month to several thousand.

What you are paying for

Strip the labels off any GEO proposal and the work falls into four parts. The price is mostly a function of how much of each you get.

  1. The audit. Your buyers' questions run on the engines that matter to you, every cited page logged, and a target list built from the pages that already decide your category. In our ChatGPT audit of one B2B category, 200 citations split into review sites 70, vendor pages 48, Reddit 42, news 12, listicles 10 and owned pages 9[1]. That list is the plan; everything else is execution.
  2. Earned placements. Getting your name onto cited pages you do not control: best-of lists, review directories, Reddit threads the engines keep retrieving, trade publishers. This is the expensive part because every page is a separate editor, moderator or community, and because the placements have to survive. It is also the part that lasts: one list in our category was cited 562 times in 30 days[1], and every citation carries the names on it.
  3. Owned pages. Rewriting your key pages so the answer is in the first paragraph, adding comparison tables and definitions, fixing schema, letting the AI crawlers in. Cheap, fast, and a small share of citations.
  4. Tracking and reporting. Daily sampling of a frozen prompt set on each engine, reported as share of voice against named competitors. Not optional: AI answers vary so much run to run that a single screenshot proves nothing[2].

What tracking costs on its own

If you want a floor for the whole thing, look at the software. Dedicated AI visibility trackers publish their prices: Peec AI[3], Otterly[4] and Semrush's AI toolkit[5] start in the low hundreds of dollars a month for a modest prompt set, and Profound's enterprise plans run into the thousands[6]. An agency fee has to cover that, plus the people reading the data and doing the placements. An agency fee that is lower than the tracking tool it claims to use is a sign it is not tracking.

What moves the price up or down

  • How many engines. Google's AI Overviews pull about 38 percent of their citations from the top 10 results[7], so if you already rank, that engine is cheaper to move. ChatGPT reads a different set of pages, and only about 12 percent of the URLs it cites rank in Google's top 10[8], so it needs its own placements.
  • How contested the category is. A niche where the engines cite three lists and two subreddits is cheaper than one where they cite thirty sources that all already have your competitors on them.
  • Reddit or not. Reddit placements need aged accounts and moderation discipline; since July 2026 Reddit removes coordinated promotion retroactively with its own language models[9]. Done properly it costs more per placement than a directory profile and lasts longer.
  • Content you already have. If your own pages are already quotable, the owned-page part is nearly free.

What it costs to do it in-house instead

One experienced hire, a tracking tool and the months they spend learning which pages matter in your category. The salary alone is usually more than a retainer, and the learning happens on your brand's accounts. The honest exception is a founder who already lives in the forums and subreddits of their niche; that person can out-place any agency. We spell the comparison out on GEO agency vs in-house.

Questions to ask before you sign

  • Which pages will you get me onto, and how many times were they cited last month? If the answer is "we will find out", the audit has not been done.
  • How many of each deliverable a month? Lists, articles, threads, directory profiles. Sites and subjects can stay open; counts cannot.
  • Which prompts are tracked, on which engines, and are they frozen for the engagement?
  • What happens at month three if nothing has moved?
  • Who posts on Reddit, from which kind of accounts, and what is removed?

Frequently asked

How much does a GEO agency cost?

Most GEO agencies charge a monthly retainer for a scoped amount of work: the audit, a set number of placements on cited pages, your own pages rebuilt, and tracking with a monthly report. At Redaccel engagements typically start around $2,000 a month, depending on your niche, with a three-month minimum.

Why is there no fixed price list?

Because the work depends on which pages the engines cite in your category and how many of them already carry your competitors. The audit answers that, and the offer follows from the audit.

What does AI visibility tracking cost without an agency?

Dedicated trackers publish their prices: the entry plans at Peec AI, Otterly and Semrush's AI toolkit sit in the low hundreds of dollars a month for a modest prompt set, and enterprise tools like Profound cost thousands. Tracking only tells you where you stand; it does not place you anywhere.

Is per-placement pricing cheaper than a retainer?

It can look cheaper for a one-off push, but it rewards landing easy pages over cited pages. Whatever the model, ask for the citation count behind each target page before you pay for it.
