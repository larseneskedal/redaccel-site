How much does GEO cost?

Updated July 2026 · Prices are real and current — no “contact us for pricing”
Extract redaccel.com · pricing · July 2026

Redaccel's GEO plans run from €500 to €5,000+ per month: Monitor (€500–800/mo) tracks your AI visibility across five engines; Growth (€1,500–2,500/mo) adds 6–10 earned placements a month; Managed (€3,000–5,000+/mo) adds PR outreach and reputation defence. Every plan has a three-month minimum, and the KPI we report is share of voice in AI answers — not rankings.

Why a three-month minimum?

Because GEO takes 6–10 weeks to show measurable movement. Engines re-crawl and re-weigh sources on their own schedules; earned placements need time to be discovered and trusted. A one-month engagement would end with a report that honestly says "too early to tell", and we'd rather lose the deal than sell that. After three months you're month-to-month.

What KPI do you actually get?

Share of voice in AI answers. Each month you see: the percentage of answers to your buyers' prompts (across five engines) that mention you versus each competitor, whether the mention was a recommendation or a passing reference, and which specific pages drove each citation. We deliberately do not report rankings — there are no rankings inside a paragraph — and we don't dress up "AI traffic estimates" nobody can verify. If share of voice isn't moving by month three, the report says so in plain language and we talk about whether to change approach or stop.

Frequently asked

Is there a setup fee?
No. The first month includes the setup work (prompt-set design, baseline measurement, placement map) at the normal monthly rate.
Can I start with Monitor and upgrade?
Yes — that's the normal path. Monitor's baseline becomes the before/after evidence for Growth.
Do you work with competitors in my category?
No. One brand per buyer-prompt set — we can't honestly maximize two clients' share of the same answers.
What's not included?
Paid ads management, link-building retainers, technical SEO and social media management. Where a problem is one of those, the report says so instead of absorbing it into scope.
