What decides the price?

Three things: how many pages are already being cited in your niche, what it costs to get onto them (some placements are earned, some are paid media), and how many engines and markets we track. We price after the audit because the audit shows exactly which pages matter, so the two options match the work your niche needs. It also tells you honestly whether you need any of this at all.

Why a three-month minimum?

GEO takes 6 to 10 weeks to show measurable movement. Engines re-read and re-weigh sources on their own schedules, and new placements need time to be found and trusted. A one-month engagement would end with a report that says "too early to tell", and we would rather lose the deal than sell that.

What KPI do you get?

Share of voice in AI answers. Each month: the share of answers to your buyers' questions, across five engines, that mention you versus each competitor, whether it was a recommendation, and which pages drove it. If it is not moving by month three, the report says so in plain words.

What is not included?

Paid ads management, link-building retainers, technical SEO and social media management. Where a problem is one of those, the report says so instead of quietly absorbing it into scope.

Why no fixed price list?

Because the work differs a lot between niches. A B2B tool with two competitors and a crowded consumer category with twenty have very different lists of cited pages, and those pages cost different amounts to get onto. The audit finds the list first, and both options are sized to it.

Is there a setup fee?

No. The first month includes the setup work (question set design, baseline measurement, placement map) at the normal monthly rate.

Can I start with the focused option and move up?

Yes. Starting with the most cited pages and adding the rest once the first results show is a sensible path. The audit's baseline stays the same, so the before and after numbers stay comparable.

Do you work with competitors in my category?

No. One brand per set of buyer questions. We cannot honestly maximise two clients' share of the same answers.
