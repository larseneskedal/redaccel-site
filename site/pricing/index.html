What decides the number?

Three things: how competitive your category is, how many engines and markets we track, and how contested your buyer prompts are.

We quote an exact figure after the free audit — which also tells you honestly whether you need any of this at all. No anchoring games: the audit shows the gap, the quote matches the work required to close it.

Why a three-month minimum?

Because GEO takes 6–10 weeks to show measurable movement. Engines re-crawl and re-weigh sources on their own schedules; earned placements need time to be discovered and trusted.

A one-month engagement would end with a report that honestly says "too early to tell", and we'd rather lose the deal than sell that. After three months you're month-to-month.

What KPI do you actually get?

Share of voice in AI answers. Each month you see the percentage of answers to your buyers' prompts — across five engines — that mention you versus each competitor, whether each mention was a recommendation or a passing reference, and which specific pages drove each citation.

We deliberately do not report rankings; there are no rankings inside a paragraph. We also don't dress up "AI traffic estimates" nobody can verify.

If share of voice isn't moving by month three, the report says so in plain language, and we talk about whether to change approach or stop.

What's the ROI of GEO?

A citation is an asset; an ad is an expense. That difference is the whole ROI case.

One more asymmetry: your category's AI answers are being written right now, from a small set of sources. Whoever occupies those sources first is the incumbent every later entrant has to displace. The cost of winning a citation surface is lowest before your competitors are trying.

Frequently asked

Is there a setup fee?
No. The first month includes the setup work (prompt-set design, baseline measurement, placement map) at the normal monthly rate.
Can I start with Monitor and upgrade?
Yes — that's the normal path. Monitor's baseline becomes the before/after evidence for Growth.
Do you work with competitors in my category?
No. One brand per buyer-prompt set — we can't honestly maximize two clients' share of the same answers.
What's not included?
Paid ads management, link-building retainers, technical SEO and social media management. Where a problem is one of those, the report says so instead of absorbing it into scope.
