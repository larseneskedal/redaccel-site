Redaccel finds the pages AI engines already cite when buyers in a niche ask what to choose, then earns the brand a place on them. That means Reddit threads, best-of lists, comparison pages, review sites, YouTube, articles and PR placements, forums, directories and the brand's own pages. Every engagement starts with a free AI visibility audit, and everything is reported as share of voice in AI answers across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews.

01 · The audit comes first

No campaign starts without the map.

The audit is the biggest step in the whole engagement, and it is free. It is where the map and the plan are built.

We take the questions your buyers actually type ("best expense tool for a 10-person agency", "[competitor] alternatives", "is [your category] worth it"), run them across the engines, and record every page each answer cites. That list of cited pages becomes the target list. Nothing goes on it because we think it might help; it is on it because the engines are already reading it.

In every audit
  • Who gets namedWhich engines name you, and which name your competitors instead.
  • The pages behind the answersEvery thread, list, review page and article the engines cite.
  • A place-by-place planWhere to show up first, most cited first.
  • Two tailored optionsA focused one and a full one, each with its price.

04 · Measure

The same questions, every month.

The question set from your audit is frozen, so every report compares like with like.

Each month you see how often each engine names you against each competitor, whether it was a recommendation or a passing mention, and which pages drove it. If nothing moved, the report says that too, in plain words.

05 · When AI gets you wrong

Fix it where the engines read it.

Engines repeat what their sources say, including outdated or hostile pages. If ChatGPT tells buyers you lack a feature you shipped last year, we trace the claim to the pages it comes from and fix it there: updated reviews, corrected articles, clear pages the engines will read again.

Ground rules

What we deliberately do not do

  • No fake reviews, no bot accounts, no undisclosed seeding.Not only an ethics position, a durability one. Platforms remove that work after the fact[1], and a removed citation is worse than none.
  • No guessing.We do not place you on sites because they look relevant. Only on pages the engines already cite for your buyers' questions.
  • No guaranteed citations.Engines are probabilistic. We guarantee the work and an honest measurement, and by month three the numbers say whether it is working.
  • No open-ended lock-in.Three-month minimum, because that is how long the work takes to show. Month to month after that.
