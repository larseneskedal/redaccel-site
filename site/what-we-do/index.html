What we do

Updated July 2026
Extract redaccel.com · services · July 2026

Redaccel does three things: measures how visible a brand is in AI answers, earns it legitimate citations across the sources engines trust, and defends its reputation when engines repeat something wrong. Everything is reported against one KPI — share of voice in AI answers across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews.

1. Monitor — see the battlefield

We build a prompt set from the questions your buyers actually type. Not keywords — prompts: "best expense tool for a 10-person agency", "is [your category] worth it", "[competitor] alternatives". Then we run it across five engines on a schedule.

Read more

The monthly report shows which engines mention you, which recommend competitors instead, and the exact pages those answers pull from. That last part is the operational gold: it converts "AI visibility" from a vibe into a to-do list.

Sold standalone as Monitor — and included in everything above it. Pricing.

2. Earn citations — become part of the consensus

Engines synthesize answers from sources where people genuinely compare things. We place your brand into that consensus legitimately: 6–10 earned placements a month, each built to survive both human moderation and the LLM-based detection platforms now run[1].

Read more

Our Reddit training is the filter. If a placement wouldn't survive a strict moderator reading it for intent, we don't make it.

3. Defend — when the answer is wrong about you

Engines repeat what their sources say, including when the sources are outdated, confused or hostile. If ChatGPT tells buyers you don't have a feature you shipped last year, or repeats a pricing complaint from 2024, that's revenue leaking through an answer you never see.

Read more

Reputation defence means tracing the wrong claim to its source pages and fixing it there — updated reviews, corrected articles, authoritative pages engines will re-read.

This plus PR outreach is the Managed layer, built for contested categories where the answer itself is a battleground.

What we deliberately don't do

  • No fake reviews, no bot accounts, no undisclosed seeding. Not (only) an ethics posture — a durability one. Platforms remove that work retroactively[1], and a removed citation is worse than none.
  • No ranking reports. There are no rankings inside a paragraph. The KPI is share of voice in AI answers.
  • No guarantees of specific citations. Engines are probabilistic. We guarantee the work and the transparency of the measurement, and by month three the numbers say whether it's working.
  • No indefinite lock-in. Three-month minimum (that's how long the physics takes), month-to-month after.
